The underbody of a car is its most exposed surface. While paint protection and ceramic coatings preserve the panels everyone can see, the chassis, sills, suspension components and wheel arches are left to absorb everything the road throws at them — unprotected.

Underbody coating changes that. A single application of Feynlab Industrial V2 bonds to metal, plastic and composite surfaces beneath your car, delivering ten years of protection against corrosion, chemical attack, abrasion and moisture ingress.

It reduces cleaning intervals, protects against salt and grime build-up, and preserves the structural integrity of components that are expensive to replace and impossible to ignore once they start to deteriorate.

The product

Feynlab
Industrial V2

Feynlab Industrial V2 is a multi-purpose, UV-resistant coating designed for maximum protection across hard surfaces — vehicle underbody, chassis, suspension components, arches, metals and plastics.

As a high-solids, single-component coating, it provides superior resistance to chemicals, abrasion and sun exposure. Extremely hydrophobic, with excellent release properties that prevent contamination from bonding to treated surfaces.

The same product applied to the underside of trains in Norway to prevent snow and ice adhesion. On your car, it means the most exposed surfaces receive the toughest protection Feynlab makes.

10-Year Durability
A single application lasts a decade. Protection that continues working long after most treatments have worn away.
Chemical Resistance
Resists road salt, brake fluid, oils, fuel and industrial contaminants. The chemistry that attacks unprotected underbodies cannot penetrate this coating.
Abrasion Resistance
Stone chips, grit and road debris bounce off rather than scoring the surface. The coating absorbs impact that would otherwise reach bare metal.
Extreme Hydrophobicity
Water and contamination bead and release rather than sitting on the surface. The underbody stays cleaner for longer between washes.
UV Resistance
Plastics and rubbers beneath the car are protected from UV degradation — preserving flexibility and preventing the cracking that accelerates deterioration.

What gets protected

Bespoke to every car
and every requirement.

I
Full Underbody

Complete coverage of the entire underside — chassis rails, floor pan, crossmembers and all structural components. Nothing underneath is left untreated.

II
Wheel Arches

The arches take more direct stone chip and abrasion impact than any other part of the car. Coating the inner arches and arch liners dramatically reduces the damage that accumulates over time.

III
Suspension Components

Wishbones, subframes, anti-roll bars, brake lines and callipers. Components that are expensive to replace and critical to safety — coated for long-term protection against corrosion and contamination.

IV
Sills & Shut Lines

The sills and door shut lines are prime entry points for moisture and corrosion. Coating these areas prevents the trapped water and salt ingress that causes rust to develop unseen.

Why it matters

The case for protecting
what you can't see.

I

Corrosion Prevention

Bare metal exposed to road salt, moisture and oxygen corrodes. Once it starts, it spreads. Underbody coating seals metal surfaces from the conditions that cause corrosion before it has a chance to begin.

II

Residual Value

A clean, protected underbody is increasingly scrutinised at point of sale — especially on prestige and classic vehicles. A car with a coated, corrosion-free underbody commands higher values and shorter sales periods.

III

Reduced Maintenance

Contamination that would otherwise bond to bare metal or plastic releases easily from a coated surface. Cleaning intervals are reduced and the condition of components is maintained for longer between services.
