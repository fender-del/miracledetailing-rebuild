Paul Dalton · The man behind the work

Since 1988.

He started at 13, washing cars for pocket money in Surrey, and founded Miracle Detail in 1994. 30+ years and 10,000+ cars later, Feynlab made him their only global ambassador.

On TV · In the papers

Fifth Gear, 2006.

The £5,000 car wash: a Maserati MC12, one of 50 ever made, in 61 stages and 64 hours. The Sunday Mirror ran the story, and a generation of British detailers still cite it as the moment they understood what the craft could become.
