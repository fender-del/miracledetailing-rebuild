A Considered Approach

The detail is in
every surface.

At Miracle Detail, interior detailing is approached with the same patience and attention as our exterior work. No two cabins are alike. Materials, age, previous treatments and condition all influence how we clean and protect them.

Whether your vehicle is a modern daily driver, a cherished classic or an exceptional collector car, we begin with an assessment and select methods appropriate to its individual surfaces.

Our aim is a beautifully clean, natural-looking interior — not an artificially shiny finish or a heavy fragrance masking the problem.

Interior Detailing Services

Cleaned with care.
Finished with precision.

Every service is tailored to the vehicle. Depending on its condition and materials, your interior detail may include the following.

01 / LEATHER

Leather Cleaning & Care

Careful removal of accumulated grime, body oils and residues from seats, bolsters, armrests and leather trim, using material-appropriate methods to preserve the original finish.

02 / FABRICS

Fabric & Carpet Cleaning

Detailed vacuuming, targeted stain treatment and controlled deep cleaning of carpets, mats and upholstery where suitable. We work to lift contamination without unnecessary saturation.

03 / FINE DETAIL

Dashboards & Intricate Trim

Vents, switches, controls, consoles, seams, crevices and hard-to-reach areas receive meticulous attention, with finishes chosen to suit the original material.

04 / FINISHES

Delicate & Specialist Materials

Alcantara, suede-effect finishes, piano black, carbon fibre, wood and sensitive heritage materials require individual assessment and appropriate specialist care.

05 / GLASS

Interior Glass & Presentation

Interior glass, mirrors, screens and visible finishing details are carefully cleaned for a crisp, refined result, with consideration for coated and sensitive displays.

06 / PROTECTION

Interior Surface Protection

Suitable leather, fabric and trim surfaces can receive specialist protection designed to make routine care easier and help reduce the impact of everyday soiling.

Long-Term Care

Protection that works
beyond collection day.

A freshly detailed cabin is only the beginning. Appropriate interior protection can help reduce staining, make dirt easier to remove and simplify future maintenance.

We assess each surface before recommending a product. Leather, fabric, carpets and interior trim have different requirements; a universal coating is not the answer.

Protection does not make an interior impervious to damage, wear, dye transfer or neglect. Good maintenance remains essential.

Frequently Asked Questions

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can you remove every stain?

No responsible specialist can guarantee that. Results depend on the stain, material, age and any previous attempts to remove it. We will explain what is realistically achievable.

Can you clean classic car interiors?

Yes. Older leather, fabrics and original finishes are assessed carefully. Preservation may be more appropriate than aggressive cleaning.

Does interior protection prevent all damage?

No. It can make cleaning and maintenance easier, but it cannot prevent physical wear, cuts, permanent dye transfer or every type of staining.

How much does an interior detail cost?

Pricing depends on the vehicle, materials, condition and scope of work. Contact us with your vehicle details and photographs for an informed quotation.
