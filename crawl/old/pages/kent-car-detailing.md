### Ceramic Coating

Ceramic coating protects your car for an extended period of time by repelling dirt, UV rays, and impurities while giving a glossy, hydrophobic finish that simplifies maintenance and retains the paint’s integrity.

### Window Tinting

Window tinting improves privacy, reduces glare, blocks dangerous UV rays, controls interior temperature, protects against fading, and gives your vehicle a sleek and attractive appearance, boosting comfort and energy efficiency.

### Dry ice and laser cleaning

Dry ice and laser cleaning are efficient and environmentally safe ways to remove rust, paint, and pollutants from surfaces, assuring a comprehensive restoration without causing harm and a clean and beautiful finish.

### Leather Restoration

Leather restoration gives old or damaged leather surfaces new life, restoring their beauty and durability. It restores coloor, repairs cracks, and extends the life of leather furniture, automobile seats, and accessories, keeping their value and improving their beauty.

### Bodyshop Repairs And Restoration

Bodyshop repairs and paint renovation restore your vehicle’s body to its original state, mending dents, scratches, and paint flaws. It improves the aesthetics, maintains the value, and assures a flawless exterior look.

### Paint Protection Film

Paint Protection Film (PPF) protects the paint on your vehicle from scratches, stone chips, and other road debris, keeping it in excellent condition. It provides a transparent layer of protection that preserves the original paintwork while increasing its lifetime.

### Car Detailing

Car detailing professionally cleans, polishes, and protects your vehicle’s interior and exterior surfaces to restore it to its former beauty. It improves the looks, preserves the value, and delivers a pleasurable driving experience.

### Mobile Detailing

Mobile detailing provides professional car care to the door, convenience, time savings, and individualised service. It ensures that your vehicle receives expert attention and meticulous detailing without the inconvenience of having to travel to a fixed location.

### Wheel Refurbishment

Wheel refurbishing is restoring damaged or worn wheels by removing scratches, dents, and kerb damage. It enhances the car’s overall appearance, adding value and style while improving the appearance and extending the lifespan of the wheels.

### Paintwork Correction

Paintwork restoration removes swirl marks, scratches, and other flaws from your vehicle’s paint, restoring its shine and depth. It improves your vehicle’s shine, clarity, and overall appearance, giving it a showroom-worthy finish.
