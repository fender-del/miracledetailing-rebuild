## What is Studio Car Valeting ?

Car valeting is a comprehensive cleaning and maintenance process for vehicles. It entails deep cleaning and protecting a vehicle to improve its appearance whether for routine upkeep or to prepare it for sale.

Car valeting includes a thorough cleaning process, addressing the interior and exterior. Our services typically include washing, waxing, interior cleaning, trim and tyre treatments, polishing and protecting.
